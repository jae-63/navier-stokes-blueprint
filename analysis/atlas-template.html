Navier–Stokes Proof Atlas

The argument

Start a fluid at rest, push it with one smooth force that is compactly supported in space and time, and the velocity becomes unbounded as t → 1 while the energy stays bounded. So no global smooth solution with bounded energy exists, which is Clay's alternative (C).

With a force allowed, any velocity field solves Navier–Stokes if you define the force as whatever the equation leaves over. The hard part is keeping that leftover smooth through and past t = 1 while the velocity blows up. A collapsing vortex supplies the blowup. Oscillatory pulses at its edge cancel the singular part of the residual through their averaged momentum flux. Repeated correction cycles clean up the rest.

In the dependency graph, that construction is about 95% of the code. The breakdown argument itself is small.

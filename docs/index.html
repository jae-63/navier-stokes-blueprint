Navier–Stokes Blueprint

In 2026 OpenAI published a proof that the Navier–Stokes equations can blow up in finite time, together with a Lean formalization of about 230,000 lines. These pages are reading aids for that formalization: they map how the proof is put together, so a person can find its main ideas before reading any Lean.

Blueprint75 key definitions and theorems, from the Clay Millennium statements down to the base flow, each with a one-line plain-language statement and its dependencies. Proof AtlasThe full dominator tree of the proof: which parts of the code exclusively support which results, organized by section of the paper.

The plain-language statements are AI-written paraphrases of the Lean code, not verified mathematics. Source, data and caveats: github.com/jae-63/navier-stokes-blueprint.
